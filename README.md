### Hi there 👋
### My nickname is Deki.

I completed a web development bootcamp at neuefische and have successfully finished my retraining as a Specialist in Application Development (Fachinformatiker Anwendungsentwicklung) at GFN.

Before that, I spent 19 years in the heating and plumbing trade, where I programmed heating systems and was the IT contact person in my company.

I believe that continuous learning, discipline, and ambition are key drivers of personal and professional success. Today I build full-stack web applications with TypeScript, React, Next.js and PostgreSQL, and I connect language models to real backends using function calling.

I am also interested in blockchain technology and want to deepen my knowledge of smart contracts and decentralized applications.

I am excited to apply my skills to real-world software projects.
